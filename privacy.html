Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

In short: Keine Eile has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. There is no server of our own, so we never receive your data. Your favourites, destination and settings stay on your iPhone. Your location is only shared with Apple and Wiener Linien where that's needed to answer your question.

Who is responsible

Sebastiaan Arnold, the developer of Keine Eile. Contact details will follow soon.

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following only on your device, in its own storage, shared with its widgets:

  • your favourite lines and the order you put them in
  • your destination (its name, icon and address) and your route preference
  • your extra time and any places you added for it
  • your settings (for example which vehicles to show)
  • the last position the app saw, so widgets and Siri still work when your iPhone can't provide one
  • a learned walking-pace factor, if you turned that on
  • a copy of the Wiener Linien stop lists

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Your location

Keine Eile asks for your location so it can find the stops near you and work out how long it takes to walk there. It uses your location while the app is open, and in its widgets and Siri answers if you allow that. It never tracks you in the background.

Your location is shared only with:

  • Apple Maps, to calculate walking times to stops and, if you choose "Use my current location", to find the street name. When you search for an address, what you type is sent to Apple's address search.
  • Wiener Linien, if you set a destination: your current position and your destination's position are sent to the Wiener Linien journey planner to find the fastest way there.

To show departures, the app asks Wiener Linien for the departures of specific stops. Those requests contain stop numbers, not your position.

Motion & Fitness (optional)

If you turn on "Learn my walking pace", the app reads your step data from the last 7 days to learn how fast you walk. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Only the resulting factor is kept, and nothing is sent anywhere. Turning the setting off deletes what was learned. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Other services

Requests to Apple and Wiener Linien are handled by them under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple privacy policy
  • Wiener Linien privacy policy

Like every app, Keine Eile is distributed through Apple's App Store, which may collect information under Apple's terms. The app itself contains no third-party code, advertising or analytics.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because the app stores your data only on your iPhone, you can view, change or delete it yourself in the app, or by deleting the app. If you have a question, contact us (contact details will follow soon). You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde).

Children

Keine Eile collects no data about anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
